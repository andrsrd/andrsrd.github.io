What happens when intelligence becomes a commodity?

Anders Rød
November 15, 2026
More

Every day when I wake up I find myself thinking about tomorrow.

I can spend countless hours ruminating trying to analyze what the future might hold on a daily basis. Especially on how to make directional decisions and to best position myself for the future.

Something thats been on my mind the last year or so, is;

If intelligence becomes a commodity, is growing your own corn really worth it?

I believe this to be a likely future, so I'm replacing the word if with when for the rest of this text.

So, when we get to a world where intelligence is abundant, using tokens to do knowledge work becomes as common as buying gas for your car,

What's the point of learning?

I'm not saying we as humans do not need to learn anything, what I'm trying to conclude here is more that; when intelligence becomes available everywhere, which enables you to do things in fields you would need to study for 10 years to gain the same amount of knowledge, what do you do?

You would have to be an idiot to think that spending 10 years in some field to learn is a good way to spend your time?

What I'm trying to solve is: what do we substitute this path with?

Looking no more than 10 years back, this path would be considered a guaranteed path to success. You would work yourself up in a company or an industry, to learn the inner workings and the mechanics and become "valuable".

This does not work anymore. So what do you pursue?

This is the issue I'm struggling to wrap my head around.

I will spend the next week resonating over this question, and next week's topic will be my reply to this.

If I'm able to answer the question and I'm confident it's the right conclusion, all that's left is action.
