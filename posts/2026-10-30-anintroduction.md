## A note on decision-making

Sometimes I try to see life from a coding standpoint, and lately I have been thinking how we make decisions in a computer program.
The challenges here is not writing the block of code that executes on the conditions, like this If-check:

`if(condition1 == true){`
`   do this`
`   }else{`
`   do that`
`}`

The problem is the conditions. *How do we define our conditions in life?*


Many of us in the western world live in a society with abundance of options.
- Should I commit to this? 
- What should I focus on? 
- Where should I spend my time to maximize the reward?
- Should I go down that path?
- Is commiting to this woman the right choice? What if someone better comes around?
- What do I want to spend my time working on?
- Is this the right career for me?


Its like beeing stuck between options gives us this inability to act. Its like we are paralyzed, unable to move.
Think about how much easier it would be if we only had one option. *"To take over dad's corner store in Bangalore"*.

Im not saying I envy a life thats predetermined by someone else, but at least I would be able to be the best store clerk there is.
That would be my destiny.

Today im paralyzed. 

I wish I would know what to pursue...

But in reality; I do know what to do. Like the great conqueror of the Aztecs said:

>"Burn the ships" - Hernán Cortés

Sometimes you have to travel the path of no return. Make a decision to pursue something knowing there is no way back. Obtain a laser focus towards one goal. Burn the ships.

When in pursuit of something great, where *"I and my companions suffer from a disease of the heart which can be cured only with gold."*, we do what we have to.
Pick up the flame, and burn the deadweight.
