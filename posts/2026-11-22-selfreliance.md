## Your intuition knows.

> No law can be sacred to me but that of my nature. - Ralph Waldo Emerson


Follow your intuition. 

It knows. 

Stop seeking answers externally.

If you seek the answer, you won't find it.

The answer hides somewhere you never go.

It hides within.


Have trust in yourself.

How can you expect to become anything of substance without trusting the one person you can't get rid of?

Have some faith.

Follow your intuition.
