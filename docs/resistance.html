What's holding us back?

Anders Rød
November 6, 2026
More

Why do we find ourselves in situations where we want to commit, but still struggle to do so?

It's like being chained to a tree with the key in our hand, and still unable to free ourselves. So why are we unable to turn the key?

The inability to make a sacrifice is what creates the resistance.

  • You can't create a presence, without risking the embarrassment of putting yourself out there.
  • You have to risk controversy when outspoken.
  • You can't avoid rejection and at the same time be an artist.

Embarrassment comes with presence. Controversy when outspoken. Rejection when creating.

It all comes with a price. A sacrifice.

You can't have both.

Mediocrity is the price you pay for indecisiveness.

"First say to yourself what you would be; and then do what you have to do." - Epictetus

Make a decision. Free yourself of the chains.
