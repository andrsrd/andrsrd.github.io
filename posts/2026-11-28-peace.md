## Peace

I genuinely believe we all are searching for peace.

Let me try and paint a picture. Think of a quiet lake with tall and beautiful mountains in the back. The lake is surrounded by dark green pine trees and there is no wind. No noise.

Now, think of yourself standing by the shore, watching this beautiful *painting* that your eyes are interpreting.

Quiet.

Not a single thought.

You do not think about the past. Or the future.

Just present in the moment.

Now; *What do you feel?*

Peace.

This might be what we are all searching for.
